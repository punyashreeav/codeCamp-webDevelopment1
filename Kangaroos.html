About Kangaroos

Kangaroos are large marsupials native to Australia. Known for their powerful hind legs, large feet, and long muscular tails, they are the only large animals to use hopping as their primary means of locomotion. Female kangaroos carry their underdeveloped young, known as joeys, in a specialized pouch until they are mature enough to survive outside.

Major Species

Red Kangaroo

The largest of all kangaroos and the largest terrestrial mammal native to Australia. They inhabit the arid and semi-arid center of the continent.

Eastern Grey Kangaroo

Known for its beautiful grey coat, this species prefers the fertile eastern parts of Australia and is often seen in open grasslands and forests.

Western Grey Kangaroo

Found across the southern part of Australia, these kangaroos are slightly smaller than their eastern cousins and possess a distinct brownish-grey fur.
